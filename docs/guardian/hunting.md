## Special Zones

Stats and rewards from monsters in Special Zones have been also reworked, making such zones meaningful. At the same time the missions attached to such zones now give useful rewards. The list of available Special Zones will vary depending on the server current Chaper, some zones will come other may disappear... you can check the specific list of available zones in the Server Progress section.

Below you can find information about the Chapter 1 available Special Zones:

### Elmoreden Tower

Conditions:

- Character Level: 60+
- Entry Limit: 1 time

Elmoreden Tower is a good place for a daily power up when it comes to EXP and Adena, as the monsters inside have increased rates, it is a good idea to use your scrolls and other consumables here to maximize the rewards!

There are 3 floors in the Elmoreden Tower, each floor is recommended for characters of different level, 60+, 70+ and 76+.

Every hour stronger monsters with a yellow title will spawn around, they give additional experience and adena when killed.

In the highest floor of the Elmoreden Tower you can find the Elmoreden Lady, an small solo/party Raid Boss, the Elmoreden Lady will respawn once every hour.

> Possible rewards: Experience, Adena, Elixir Powder.

### Transcendent Instance Zone

Conditions:

- Character Level: 60+
- Entry Limit: 1 time

This is a solo instance where the player has to survive to the monster waves which spawns continuously.

While inside the instance some of your skills will be replaced by "Transcendent" skill, which make damage in a wide area, helping you to kill the monster waves.

The purpose of this zone is to acquire a high amount of experience in a short period of time
> Possible Rewards: Experience, Random Craft points, Consumables.

### Alligator Island

Conditions:

- Character Level: 70
- Time Limit: 1 hour

In the alligator island you will find 2 hunting grounds, one for players level 70 or above and another for players level 85 and above. Monsters in this area doesn't give a large amount of experience or drops but they drop a lot of Adena. Additionally you can earn rewards for completing the attached mission to this zone.

> Possible Rewards: Brooch, Ruby, Emerald, Aquamarine, Opal, Amber

### Forgotten Garden

Conditions:

- Character Level: 76
- Time Limit: 3 hours

The Forgotten Garden is divided in 3 zones: Garden of Eva, Garden of Speed and Garden of Authority, the time limit is shared between the 3 zones.

You can acquire Talisman Bracelet and the Talisman of Eva, Speed and Authority in this zone.

There are some Raid Bosses that can spawn in this zones, they spawn once every hour and they can be challenged as solo/party:

- Garden of Eva: Raid Boss Ifa.
- Garden of Speed: Raid Bosses Tanas and Kadin.
- Garden of Authority: Raid Boss Radin.

> Possible Rewards: Talisman Bracelet, Talisman of Eva, Talisman of Speed, Talisman of Authority


### Training Zone

Conditions:

- Character Level: 76
- Entry Limit: 1 time

The Training Zone is a solo instance where players can choose between several difficulties based on existing open field regions such as Plains of Lizardmen, Bee Hive...

Once inside monsters will continuously spawn and after some time a Raid Boss will appear, defeating the Raid Boss is the main goal of this zone.

> Possible Rewards: Venir's Talisman Fragments

### Catacombs

Conditions: 
- Character level: 60
- Time Limit: 3 hours

Catacombs have been introduced as an Special Zone, players can visit the Catacombs and kill the monsters inside to Acquire Seal Stones which can be exchanged for Ancient Adena, a currency used to acquire Spellbooks, Magic Tablets, Guardian Spellbooks, Expanded Potential of Dyes... and also useful to complete Collections.

> Possible Rewards: Seal Stones, Ancient Adena

### World Boss Contest

Conditions:

- Character Level 70
- Entry Limit: unlimited
- Times: 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00

World Boss Contest is a Match Making Special Zone that will open 4 times per day at specific hours. 5 minutes before the zone opens players can register to participate.

Once inside the server will distribute players among 3 teams and they will challenge a powerful Epic Boss:

- Pve Contest:
    - Queen Ant
    - Core
    - Orfen
    - Zaken
- Pvp Contest:
    - Chaotic Queen Ant
    - Chaotic Core
    - Chaotic Orfen
    - Chaotic Zaken

The contest will last 10 minutes and teams must try to make the highest possible damage to the Raid Boss during that time, the boss is randomly selected, sometimes it can be a regular boss and other times a chaotic one, if a chaotic one appears the field will be a PVP zone.

All the players who participate will receive a "World Boss Contest Rune - 4 hour" which increase EXP/Adena acquisition. Additionally winners will receive a buff which temporarily increase their stats (the buff is different depending on the boss challenged).

Participating in the World Boss Contest 7 time in a week will allow you to complete its weekly mission which rewards the necessary items to craft Epic Jewels.

## Dungeons

Dungeons from different Essence versions have been adapted and reimplemented, the available dungeons will vary based on the server Chapter, below you can find the list of available Dungeons during the Chapter 1:

### Dwelling of Spirits

Conditions:

- Character level: 76
- Entry Limit: 1/week
- Party Size: 15-40

Players can visit the Dungeon Manager NPC in the Town of Aden to particiapte in the Dwelling of Spirits.

Dwelling of spirits consists in a Raid Boss challenge where players need to defeat monsters to open the Raid Boss portal, a total of 4 Raid Bosses must be defeated to complete the dungeon: Procella, Petram, Ignis and Nebula.

> Possible Rewards: Agathion Bracelet, Agathion Ignis, Agathion Nebula, Agathion Petram, Agathion Procella, Agathion Joy.

### Battle with Beoro

Conditions:

- Character level: 76
- Entry Limit: 1/week
- Party Size:15-40

Every week players can group to challenge the Raid Boss Beoro, this Raid Boss can only be damaged with your Guardians, be sure you gear them and train them properly before challenging it!

> Possible Rewards: Guardian Necklace / Contract Package.

### Dreamland

- Character level: 76
- Entry limit: 1/week
- Party Size: 5 - 15

The Dreamland dungeon has been simplified, (Beast farm and Cats have been removed). In this dungeon players must kills monsters to progress a cross the the rooms and find the Raid Boss, once killed the dungeon is completed.

> Possible Rewards: Elemental Evolution Stones.

### Clan Arena

Conditions:

- Character Level: 60
- Entry limit: 1/week
- Party Size: 15 - 40

Players from the same clan can form a group of up to 40 players to contest the Clan Arena. Once inside a Raid Boss will spawn, if defeated the next week an stronger Raid Boss will spawn, there are up to 30 difficulties in the Clan Arena, as a clan completed its stages it will be able to unlock higher levels of the clan passive skill bonus.

> Possible Rewards: Blood Crystals (necessary for Clan Halls), Consumables.

### Clan Dungeon

Conditions:

- Character Level: 76
- Entry limit: unlimited
- Party Size: 15 - 40

Clan Dungeon is a weekly contest where all clans participate in a Raid Boss challenge, each week a different Raid Boss will be challenged. Clans can rank based on the time required to complete the Clan Dungeon.

Available Raid Bosses have been completely reworked, now players will challenge Epic Bosses with complex mechanics and strong stats, as new chapters arrive, new Epic Bosses will be added to the Clan Dungeon

> Possible Rewards: Scroll Enchant: Rare Accessory

## Other Content

Among the Open Field Hunting Grounds, Special Zones and Dungeons you will find some "events" in the game which happen at specific times. In each chapter new events will arrive and others will disappear, keeping the game fresh and engaing.

Below you can find the list of available events during the Chapter 1:

### Keber

Keber has returned to Elmoreden!

Every hour cursed monsters will spawn in Plains of Glory, War-Torn Plains and Silent Valley, commanded by Keber.

When the event starts a announcement and a 10 minute timer will appear in your screen if you are in the aboved mentioned areas, monsters will spawn from the red vortex located in such zones and the Raid Boss Keber will command them.

Kill monsters and assist other players to defeat Keber to earn rewards

> Possible Rewards: A Grade Recipes and Materials to craft A grade Weapons, Armors and Accessories

### Boss Extermination

Every day at 19:00, the Boss Extermination event begins.

Battlefield Phase:

Before the Raid Boss appears, waves of monsters spawn across the battlefield. Defeating these monsters rewards Event Points.
During the event, the Top 10 players are displayed with a ranking icon above their character based on their current score.
Once the Raid Boss appears, regular monsters stop spawning.

Boss Battle: The event can take place in one of two different modes.

- Common Mode
    - One of the following Raid Bosses will appear:
        - Ekimus, Immortal Lord
        - Tiat, Maiden of Darkness
        - Baylor, Arbiter of Darkness
        - Crook, Dungeon Messenger
        - Harp, Captive Hell Demon
- Special Mode
    - With a certain chance, the event enters Special Mode. In this mode, Istina Queen appears once players collectively earn enough Event Points.

After defeating the main Raid Boss in either mode, there is also a chance for Burial Beetle Battlefield Hunter to spawn. This bonus boss can only be killed within 3 minutes.

Rewards: 

Players of Level 60+ who earn at least 1,000 Event Points are eligible for rewards.

Rewards must be claimed before 21:30. Any unclaimed rewards will disappear.

At the end of the event, players are ranked by the total number of Event Points earned. The Top 30 players receive additional rewards via in-game mail.

Winner's Treasure Chest

The player who lands the final blow on the Raid Boss receives an additional treasure chest depending on which boss was defeated.

- Ekimus, Immortal Lord → Ekimus' Treasure Chest (Sealed)
- Tiat, Maiden of Darkness → Tiat's Treasure Chest (Sealed)
- Baylor, Arbiter of Darkness → Baylor's Treasure Chest (Sealed)
- Crook, Dungeon Messenger → Crook's Treasure Chest (Sealed)
- Harp, Captive Hell Demon → Harp's Treasure Chest (Sealed)
- Istina Queen → Istina's Treasure Chest (Sealed)

If you do not obtain a Treasure Chest, you will receive a Soulshot Ticket (Sealed).

Lantern Rewards:

Each successful hit on the Raid Boss has a chance to grant a Lantern.

- Common Mode: up to 30 Lanterns.
- Special Mode: up to 100 Lanterns.

Additional Drops:

Landing the final blow on a Titled Monster or a Raid Boss has a chance to reward one of the following items:

- Augmenting Stone - Weapon
- Augmenting Stone - Armor
- Augmenting Stone - Accessory
- Augmenting Stone - Special Equipment

Damage Contribution:

You do not need to land the final hit to earn valuable rewards.

Players who deal enough damage to the Raid Boss will also receive rewards based on their contribution, making active participation worthwhile for everyone.

> Additional Rewards: Red Lantern, Hardin Soul Crystal.

### Giran Relic Defense

During the Giran Castle Siege an special event will start (10 minutes after the castle siege starts).

Near the Giran Castle entrance the Giran Relic of Protection will spawn, the Relic Keepers will try to defend it against the Immortal Kasin's Bone Legion.

If players succeed on defending it, the Giran Relic of Protection will reward all the players with a buff (Relic of Protection Blessing) which last for 6 hours. Additionally when Kasin is defeaten a treasure box will spawn, players that participate in the event can open it to acquire various rewards (random craft points, consumables...)
