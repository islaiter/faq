# Reborn: Guardian

Reborn Essence Guardian is perfect for players who love Lineage but have limited time. With offline Auto Hunt and solo-friendly progression, you can keep growing even while away and enjoy all the content with just a couple of hours of active play each day.

## Character Progression

The L Coins and Giran Seals have been removed from the game, Adena is the currency which will be used to purchase groceries and any other spenses that the game requires, while in the original Essence everything spins around L -Coins, the L-Coin Store, Special craft, Loot boxes, Offers.... Guardian server have been designed in a more traditional way, where items drop from monsters or are acquired by completing game content.

Guardian server is running on the last client version (Echo of Elements) but the game content and overall progression have been modified, the game content will be released gradually in the form of "Chapters" (similar to Chronicles). Each new chapter will bring an update to the game, introducing new content such as items, zones, levels... so you can enjoy all the game step by step.

So, killing monsters and completing game content, as it should be, for more info on item acquisition, visit [Item Acquisition](items.md)

## Sayha Grace

The Sayha Grace system has been completely reworked to mitigate the gap within different classes and avoid big differences when it comes to the amount of adena that a player can farm each day. As the Sayha Grace gauge depletes your adena drop rate will decrease as follows

| Sayha Grace Gauge | Adena Drop |
|  |  | 
| Sayha 75% or above | 100% | 
| Sayha 25% to 75% | 80% | 
| Sayha 1% to 25% | 40% | 
| Sayha 0% | 20% | 

The amount of adena dropped by monsters have been modified (increased) in multiple zones, and the prices of the items have been adjusted taking into consideration the Sayha Grace system, players won't feel any lack of adena, but instead we will prevent continuous inflation on the server and mitigate already mentioned gap within different classes.

You can refill your sayha grade in the Game Assistant. It will allow you to refill manually once a day or you can also set it to automatic mode, and then your sayha will be refilled during the daily reset at 06:30 UTC.

## Channel System

One of the great additions to our server is the Channel System. If you are familiar with Essence you already know how frustrating it can be to wake up in the morning and find out that your character have been killed and you lost an entire night of farming, or how bad it is when you arrive to a hunting ground and it is full of players so you can barely kill any monsters.

This feature is the solution for all those annoying situations, players can now freely switch through different channels, that will be dynamically created based on the amount of players in each hunting ground or instance, and this way you will always find an spot with less players.

We have created 3 type of channels:

| Type | Player Kills | Class Restriction | Entre Fee | Entry Remain | Re-entry Time
|  |  | |  |  | | 
|Regular | Not allowed | Any | 0 | - | 0 |
|Class Based | Not allowed | Melee Only | 0 | - | 0 |
| Chaotic	 | Allowed | Any | 0 | -	 | 5 min |

## Orc Fortress Siege

The Orc Fortress has been completery redesigned. Now it is a Clan PVP competition where any clan can particiapte without previous registration.

Owning the fortress grants some perks to the clan members:

- Residence Blessing Lv. 2: +20% Acquired XP/SP.
- 3 million of adena, automatically split among all clan members.
- Fortress Manager NPC will offer basic consumables at a cheaper price

### Clan Halls

Clan residences have been brought back to the game, now clans can bid to participaten in the Clan Hall Auction and rent a Clan Hall.

Clan halls are rented, not owned, clans which win a auction can enjoy the benefits of the clan hall for 4 weeks.

The bids and weekly clan hall maintenance are paid with Blood Crystals which can be acquired in the Clan Arena weekly dungeon

Clan Halls provide a buff "Residence Blessing Lv. 1" which increases acquired EXP/SP by 10%.

## Other Features

### Item Restrictions

In Reborn Essence private stores, trade and mailing are disabled and all the items are bound to the character. The game is designed to progress mostly individually when it comes to the Idle farming and to cooperate with other players (or fight them) in dedicated content such as instances, dungeons, sieges, olympiads, events, epics bosses... where rewards are distributed per player, so there is no need for transactions at all.

While it may sounds strange compared to traditional versions, in essence this is a must, since it is possible to auto-hunt, if this restrictions would not exist, players would create dozens of characters and then send all the drops and adenas to one single character, progressing 10 or 20 times faster than other players, which is one of the biggest problems in Essence. Additionally this restrictions prevent any kind of real money trading with items, which is also something very positive.

### Offline Auto-hunt

Essence counts with a built-in auto-hunt mode, which allows the players to continue killing monsters while being AFK. We have also added the "Offline auto-hunt" feature to the game, which allows you to close the game while auto-hunting, so your computer doesn't need to be turned on 24/7.

In case of a server restart or maintenance, offline auto-hunt players are automatically reconnected to the game, so you don't have to worry about it! You can turn on the offline auto-hunt from the Game Assistant menu.

### Guaranteed Success

Say good bye to the extreme RNG with the Guaranteed Success System. Each time you fail an upgrade, the guaranteed success gauge will increase and while it reaches the 100% the next upgrade attempt will always succeed.

This system is available for the following upgrades:

- Item Enchant
- Item Compound
- Special Craft
- Pattern Upgrade

### Smart Augmenting

We have redesigned the Augmenting system, now it is possible to choose which augment you are looking for and the system will keep rolling augments until it finds the wished one. You will be asked to replace your existing your augment by a new one instead of doing it automatically so you will never lose a valuable augment effect.

### Class Change

Class Change system has also been improved, now when you change your class you will have the choice to automatically refund all the spellbooks and magic tablets. Skill enchant must be extracted at the NPC before doing a Class Change to avoid any loss.

Class Change will be available once per Chapter.

### Aden Laboratory

Now it is possible to drop Aden Exploration Reports from the monsters in the open field.

The costs have been highly decreased, making Aden Laboratory worthy and a helpful feature during the early-mid game stage.

### Random Craft

From now on the random craft will be the main source of items to complete the collections. You can randomly acquire low grade items and Master Collection Marks from it, which will allow you to passively progress in your collections.

Random Craft Points can be acquired from the following sources and methods:

- Using low grade items as materials.
- Using Random Craft Charging Point Stones.
- Killing Monsters in the Transcendent Instance Zone.
- Using Asofes, Thons and Enrias dropping from monsters.

## Continous catch up

One of the best features of Guardian server is its Continuous Catch-up system which allows new players to jump into the server anytime without staying behind.

In essence it is very hard to catch up with old players due to the game design, it can take months to be near to an old player progress, and even after that you may stay behind.

How will the Continunous Catch-up system work?

- Main questline: leveling up to previous Chapter level cap (few hours)
- Chapter Starter Kit: players will acquire an starter kit with all the items/progress acquired by other players during the previous chapter (gears, enchants, dyes, hidden power stage, elixirs, skills, collections... litarelly everything!)
- Catch-up Channel: during several days players will have access to a channel exclusive for new players with increased Adena rates

What does this translate to?

- Lets say the server is 2 weeks into the Chapter 3 (server is some months old)
- Level Cap in the chapter 2 was 88 (previous chapter), players in the server are level 89+ currently
- Max items enchant in Chapter 2 was "Circlet of Hero +6" (just one example)
- Hidden power max stage was stage 10 in the Chapter 2...
- Epic Jewels max enchant was +2....
- More things...

A new player will have main questline up to level 88, so within few hours he will be just 1 level behind all the other players. He will also be able to buy "Chapter 3 starting kit" for few adenas in the store and he will get the above said items, staying just an step behind the players.

But hey! That player is still a little bit behind no? Worry not, you just jumped in the Chaper 3, and by the end of it you would've been able to complete all the content like all the other players and be at the same level and gears as they are.

This way, new players are always welcome into the server, since each new Chapter is like an small fresh start for everyone, and we avoid the need of doing "new servers", "catch ups" and "merges", which are way more disturbing and less effective.
